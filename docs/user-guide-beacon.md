# Beacon User Guide

**Elementary Counselor Command Center**

Version 1.0 | Clear Path Education Group, LLC
Support: support@clearpathedgroup.com

---

## Quick Start

1. **Open Beacon** in your browser at your district's Beacon URL (or locally if using Local mode).
2. **Choose your data mode**: Local (on-device, privacy-first) or Cloud (district-managed with sync).
3. **Activate your license** by entering the key provided by your district or purchase confirmation.
4. **Set up your school year** dates and schedule blocks in Settings.
5. Start by adding students to your **Caseload** -- then log sessions, track groups, and monitor your SB 179 compliance from the Dashboard.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Data Storage Modes

Beacon offers two modes -- choose during setup or change later in Settings.

| Mode | How It Works | Best For |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Local** | All data stored on your device. Nothing leaves your computer. | Privacy-first, single counselor, no IT setup needed |
| **Cloud** | Data syncs to your district's cloud database. | Multi-counselor districts, admin oversight, cross-device access |

In **Local mode**, always back up your data regularly (see Section 9).

### License Activation

1. Go to **Settings > License**.
2. Enter your license key.
3. Click **Activate**.
4. Your license status and expiration date appear on the Settings page.

### School Year Settings

1. Go to **Settings > School Year**.
2. Set your **start date** and **end date**.
3. These dates drive compliance tracking and reporting periods.

## 2. Dashboard

The Dashboard is your daily command center. It shows:

- **SB 179 Compliance Meter**: Your current direct/indirect service time ratio (target: 80% direct).
- **Today's Schedule**: Sessions and groups scheduled for today.
- **Caseload Summary**: Total students, active referrals, groups in progress.
- **Action Items**: Overdue session logs, pending referrals, follow-ups due.

---

## 3. Student Caseload

### Adding Students

1. Go to **Caseload** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add Student**.
3. Enter the student's name, grade, teacher, and any relevant notes.
4. Click **Save**.

### Student Profiles

Each student profile shows:

- Contact information and grade level.
- Session history (individual and group).
- Progress ratings over time.
- Counselor notes.
- Referral history.

---

## 4. Session Logging

### Individual Sessions

1. Open a student's profile.
2. Click **Log Session**.
3. Select the **date**, **duration**, and **session type** (individual, crisis, check-in, etc.).
4. Choose or type the **topic/focus area**.
5. Add **notes** and a **progress rating** (1-5 scale).
6. Click **Save**.

### Group Sessions

1. Go to **Groups** in the sidebar.
2. Select the group (or create a new one -- see Section 5).
3. Click **Log Group Session**.
4. Set the **date**, **duration**, and **topic**.
5. Mark which students **attended**.
6. Add notes and progress ratings per student (optional).
7. Click **Save**.

All sessions count toward your SB 179 compliance time tracking automatically.

---

## 5. Small Group Tracking

### Creating a Group

1. Go to **Groups > New Group**.
2. Name the group and select the **focus area** (anxiety, social skills, grief, etc.).
3. Add students from your caseload.
4. Set the **planned number of sessions** and **frequency**.
5. Click **Save**.

### Managing Groups

- Track attendance and progress per session.
- View completion percentage (sessions completed vs. planned).
- Close groups when the series is finished.

## 6. Lesson Library

Beacon includes **35 built-in lessons** covering common elementary counseling topics.

1. Go to **Lessons** in the sidebar.
2. Browse by category (feelings, friendship, bullying, self-regulation, etc.).
3. Click a lesson to view the full plan.
4. Use lessons in individual or group sessions -- link them when logging a session.

## 7. Referral Management

### Manual Referrals

1. Go to **Referrals > New Referral**.
2. Select or add the **student**.
3. Enter the **referring teacher**, **reason**, and **urgency level**.
4. Click **Submit**.

### Google Form Import (CSV)

For schools using Google Forms for teacher referrals:

1. **Set up your Google Form** with fields for student name, teacher, reason, and date.
2. Add a Google Apps Script to save responses to a Google Sheet (template available from support).
3. **Download the sheet as CSV**.
4. In Beacon, go to **Referrals > Import**.
5. Upload the CSV and map columns.
6. Review and confirm the import.

### Referral Workflow

New referrals appear in your **Referrals** list. For each referral:

1. **Review** the details.
2. **Accept** to add the student to your active caseload.
3. **Log sessions** as you work with the student.
4. **Close** the referral when resolved.

## 9. SB 179 Compliance Tracking

Senate Bill 179 requires elementary counselors to spend at least 80% of their time on direct student services.

### How Beacon Tracks Compliance

- Every logged session (individual, group, classroom lesson) counts as **direct service time**.
- Administrative tasks, meetings, and planning count as **indirect time**.
- Beacon calculates your ratio automatically based on logged activities.

### Viewing Your Compliance

- The **Dashboard** shows your current compliance percentage.
- Go to **Reports > SB 179** for detailed breakdowns by week, month, or year.
- The compliance threshold is configurable in **Settings** (default: 80%).

---

## 10. Schedule Blocks

Organize your weekly schedule:

1. Go to **Settings > Schedule Blocks**.
2. Add recurring blocks for groups, classroom lessons, lunch duty, meetings, etc.
3. Blocks appear on your Dashboard and help you plan your week.
4. Each block can be tagged as direct or indirect service for compliance tracking.

---

## 11. Data Backup & Restore (Local Mode)

Since Local mode stores data only on your device, regular backups are essential.

### Creating a Backup

1. Go to **Settings > Data**.
2. Click **Export Backup**.
3. Save the JSON file to a secure location (USB drive, cloud storage, etc.).

### Restoring from Backup

1. Go to **Settings > Data**.
2. Click **Import Backup**.
3. Select your JSON backup file.
4. Confirm the restore. This replaces all current data.
