# Beacon — Facebook Video Script

**Voice:** Kim Culley, first person — sitting practitioner, warm but specific. No consultant-speak.
**Brand colors on titles / lower-thirds:** teal/cyan #0a2e2a → #2A9D8F → #2dd4bf (matching beacon.clearpathedgroup.com).
**CTA destination:** `https://clearpathedgroup.com` — the marketing-site homepage. The `/store.html` Buy Now is the conversion target; the homepage Beacon section answers the "is this real" question first.
**Targeting:** Texas elementary counselor pages, regional ESC counselor groups, TSCA / TCA Facebook groups.
**Captions:** required. Most counselors watch with sound off in the lounge / between bells.

---

## A. 60-second main script (primary deliverable)

> **0:00 — 0:05 | HOOK** (Kim, talking head — counselor's office or library backdrop)
>
> "It's Tuesday at 7:55. There's three teacher referrals in your inbox, a parent you forgot to call back, your SB 179 time log is overdue, and the bell hasn't rung yet."
>
> **B-roll:** quick cuts — a sticky note pile, a watch reading 7:55, a phone ringing, an Outlook inbox with red dots.

> **0:05 — 0:18 | RECOGNITION** (Kim, slightly tighter shot)
>
> "I'm Kim Culley. I work in Texas K-12 schools. I built Beacon because I watched elementary counselors hold the line for every kid who walked through their door — and then go home and do the paperwork at 9 PM. That's not sustainable. And that's not why you got into this."
>
> **B-roll:** a counselor's desk at night, a kid's drawing pinned to a corkboard, a partially-graded check-in form.

> **0:18 — 0:48 | SHOW** (screen recording of Beacon, voiced over)
>
> "Beacon is one place for your caseload, your groups, your referrals, your crisis logs, and your SB 179 time — all on your device.
>
> Teacher needs to send you a referral? Print one QR poster, hang it in the workroom, every submission lands in your queue.
>
> Crisis just walked in? One button starts a structured suicide screen, threat assessment, or CPS workflow with auto-scheduled 24-hour, 72-hour, and one-week follow-ups. You will not lose track of a kid because you got pulled into another crisis.
>
> SB 179? Every session you log auto-categorizes against the 80/20 split. You see your ratio in real time, not the day before your evaluation."
>
> **Screen-rec sequence (≈8s each):**
> 1. Referrals queue with the "Share Form" QR poster, then an inbound referral arriving in the queue
> 2. Crisis Quick-Launch button → 14-step suicide screen → "Follow-up scheduled for Thursday" confirmation
> 3. SB 179 dashboard with 80/20 ring + this-week breakdown

> **0:48 — 0:55 | TRUST** (Kim, talking head)
>
> "Beacon was built by a sitting administrator. Not a tech company. It's been counselor-tested. And your student data stays on your device — no district DPA required to start."
>
> **B-roll:** a "Local Mode — Your Data Stays Here" indicator pill from the Beacon UI; the Texas state outline.

> **0:55 — 1:00 | CTA** (Kim, full screen with title card)
>
> "Try it free for 14 days. $79 a year if you stay. Beacon dot clearpathedgroup dot com."
>
> **Title card:** `Beacon — for Texas elementary counselors. Free 14-day trial. $79/year.`
> Lower CTA: `clearpathedgroup.com` (large, teal underline).

Use the 60-second script above and insert one extra beat between RECOGNITION and SHOW:

>

## A/B test alternative hooks (swap into the 0:00–0:05 slot)

1. **(default)** "It's Tuesday at 7:55. Three referrals, a parent call, your SB 179 time log overdue — and the bell hasn't rung."
2. "Raise your hand if you've ever forgotten to follow up on a 24-hour suicide check-in. Now imagine that came up at trial."
3. "Show me the elementary counselor whose paper referral binder hasn't been the source of a 'we lost that one' conversation."
4. "Your job is to be there for every kid. The tools they gave you are a binder and a spreadsheet. We can do better than that."

Test in pairs against the same body + CTA. Hook 2 has the highest emotional intensity but skews to lawsuit-fear; hook 1 is the safest opener for the broadest counselor audience. Hook 4 is the closest to the brand-email voice already in production.

## TEC accuracy notes (do not skip)

- **SB 179 = 2019 counselor 80/20 bill (TEC §33.006).** Do not conflate with SB 179 of 2017 (cyberbullying / David's Law / TEC §37.0832). Per DECISIONS.md 2026-04-26 — every TEC reference verified against current statutory text before shipping any customer-facing material.
- **"Texas elementary counselors"** is a verified TAM phrasing — this is who Beacon is built for. Avoid generic "K-12 counselors" framing in this video; the SB 179 hook is Texas-specific.
